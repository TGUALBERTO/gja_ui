**Reason**: `RebicRow` dá lugar a `RebicHBox`, como o `sap.m.HBox` do SAPUI5.
**Migration**: Use `RebicHBox`: `children` passa a `items`, `mainAlignment` a `justifyContent` (`RebicFlexJustifyContent`), `crossAlignment` a `alignItems` (`RebicFlexAlignItems`) e `shrinkWrap` a `displayInline`; `RebicRow.wrap` passa a `RebicHBox(wrap: RebicFlexWrap.wrap, alignItems: RebicFlexAlignItems.start)`.
